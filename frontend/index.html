How SmartCare Queue AI Works

From registration to consultation, our intelligent system ensures zero waiting room congestion.

1️⃣

Select Department

Choose from General Medicine, Cardiology, Pediatrics, Orthopedics, or Diagnostics.

2️⃣

Get Digital Token

Receive an instant secure QR token with real-time wait estimation powered by AI.

3️⃣

Track from Anywhere

Relax at the cafeteria or wait from home while receiving live queue notifications.

4️⃣

Indoor Navigation

Follow step-by-step hospital floor maps right to your doctor's consultation counter.

Next-Gen Hospital Tech

Comprehensive Digital Healthcare Platform

Designed specifically for patients, doctors, and hospital operations staff.

⚡

Concurrency-Safe Queues

Atomic PostgreSQL token increments prevent duplicate ticket numbers even under massive concurrent patient traffic.

🤖

AI Wait-Time Prediction

Calculates dynamic wait durations using active doctors, service load, historical duration, and congestion multipliers.

🕒

Best Time to Visit

Identifies low-traffic windows (e.g., 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM) to distribute outpatient arrivals and prevent overcrowding.

🗺️

Interactive Hospital Map

Indoor wayfinding with waypoint routing, walking time calculations, and multi-floor path guidance.

💳

Online Simulated Billing

Digital fee breakdowns, one-click simulated UPI settlements, and instantaneous receipt generation.

🔬

Diagnostic Lab Tracker

Four-stage status tracking (Ordered, Sample Collected, Processing, Report Ready) with automated patient alerts.
